A MESSAGE FROM MY HEART

For Emelda ❤️

Some feelings are difficult to explain.
Some words take courage to say.
And some people become special without even knowing it. 🌹

Dear Emelda, ❤️

There is something I have been carrying in my heart for quite some time, something I have tried so hard to keep to myself. But honestly, I don't think I can continue pretending that my feelings are just ordinary.

From the very first day we met, the day you reported to Karatina, something about you caught my attention. I remember how nervous I was. I wanted to open up to you, to tell you what was happening inside my heart, but somehow I couldn't find the courage.

Every time I thought about telling you, I would imagine a hundred different things. What if you didn't feel the same? What if things became awkward? What if I ruined something beautiful before it even had a chance to begin?

So I kept quiet. I tried to control my feelings, convince myself to wait, and act as though everything was normal. But the truth is, Emelda, my feelings for you have continued growing.

Sometimes I find myself smiling at the smallest thoughts of you. Sometimes I wish I could simply tell you everything without worrying about how it might sound. And sometimes I wonder whether you have ever noticed just how special you have become to me.

Today, I have decided to be honest with you. Not because I expect anything from you, but because I believe some feelings deserve to be expressed rather than hidden forever.

I like you, Emelda. Truly. ❤️

And if your heart ever feels something similar, I would love the opportunity to get to know you in a deeper, more beautiful way.

I would love to share laughter with you, meaningful conversations, little moments, beautiful memories, and perhaps a story that neither of us expected to begin.

But most importantly, I want you to know that your feelings matter too. You deserve honesty, kindness, patience, and the freedom to express exactly what is in your heart.

Whatever your response may be, thank you for being someone who inspired me to write these words.

With a heart that finally found its courage,
Someone who likes you more than words can explain. 💗

What does your heart say? 🌹
